# Soundpack research and agent handoff

## Goal and status

Expand the user's Ableton library with tech house, deep house, UKG, and hardgroove sounds. The user requested broad research, a candidate proposal, a repository handoff, and some downloads.

Recommendations below reflect product descriptions, contents, artist provenance, and workflow—not listening tests. Prices are research snapshots, not verified checkout quotes. Recheck availability, licence, exact edition, Live version, and plugin requirements before purchase. “Inspired by” is not an artist collaboration.

Two official free Wave Alchemy tasters have been downloaded to `artifacts/soundpacks/`. These are production samples, not full commercial tracks or purchased packs. See `docs/research/soundpacks/download-manifest.json` for sources, checksums, audio counts, and local paths (also copied beside the downloads). The two archives passed ZIP integrity checks and were extracted: 38 WAVs from Deep House & Garage and 55 WAVs from Deep Tech House. `artifacts/` is git-ignored, so audio stays local and will not accompany a clone; the tracked manifest preserves download URLs for another agent. No purchase or account creation was performed.

## Next agent: execution plan

1. Read the download manifest and inspect the included licence PDFs before using samples in released music. Completion: each imported pack has a known source and applicable licence.
2. Audition the downloaded WAVs in Live. Keep original archives and documentation. Completion: report which sounds suit the user's tracks; do not equate sample count with quality.
3. Audition the first-choice basket below through official product demos. Completion: recommend a small, complementary set, noting overlap and required plugins.
4. Obtain user approval for specific paid packs and a spending limit before purchasing. Use the user's authorized accounts only. Free downloads that require an email or login should be reported for user action.
5. If the user means finished reference tracks rather than production samples, ask for artists, track preferences, and budget. Use authorized downloads; streaming previews are not production assets.
6. For further downloads, record source page, actual download URL, local file, checksum, contents, and licence status. Validate archives before extraction and keep paths within the chosen download directory.

## First-choice audition basket

- Tech/minimal: East End Dubs and Tony Romera Vol. 2.
- Deep house: Wave Alchemy Deep House & Garage and Mad Zach's Deep House Lab.
- UKG: El-B and Niche Audio 2 Step Garage, preferably its Ableton edition.
- Hardgroove: Abstract Hardgroove and SINEE Essentials Vol. 1, or its bundle if pricing makes sense.

These are audition priorities, not approval to buy all eight.
